> "Quick show of hands — how many of you have used an AI coding assistant in the last week? [wait] In the last month? [wait] Never? [wait] Interesting. Today I want to talk about something that's changing that ratio rapidly..."

## 🎬 CLOSING STRONG

### Call to Action
> "Here's what I want you to do: This week, pick ONE task — writing tests, documentation, a simple utility — and try vibe coding it. See for yourself. And then let's talk about what you learned. The future of development is being shaped right now, and we should be part of shaping it."

### If Q&A is Quiet
Have these prompts ready:
- "What concerns do you have about this approach?"
- "Has anyone tried these tools? What was your experience?"
- "What tasks in your current work might benefit from this?"
- "What would you need to see to feel comfortable using this?"

## 🔥 HANDLING DIFFICULT MOMENTS

### If Someone Disagrees Strongly
> "That's a valid perspective. There are definitely scenarios where traditional coding makes more sense. The key is knowing when to use which approach. Would love to hear more about your specific concerns after the session."

### If You Don't Know an Answer
> "Great question — I don't have a definitive answer for that. Let me look into it and follow up with you. Anyone else have thoughts on this?"

### If Technical Issues Occur
- Have presentation PDF as backup
- Know your content well enough to present from notes
- Stay calm: "Give me just a moment..."

### If Someone Dominates Q&A
> "Great points — let's make sure we hear from others too. [Name], did you have thoughts on this?"
